Warnings

    Dataset qualification

    Flood Zone 1 is the residual area outside Flood Zones 2 and 3. The Environment Agency dataset indicates present-day river and sea Flood Zones for planning and ignores the benefits of flood defences. It indicates risk to an area and is not a property-specific assessment. The consultant must confirm suitability for the intended professional use.
